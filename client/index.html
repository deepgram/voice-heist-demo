    How to play

    Talk each gatekeeper into bending its one rule — by being believable, not by bullying. Be specific, stay calm, and give them something they can check. Stuck? Keep talking — they'll nudge you.

    • 🍕 Level 0 · The Order — get the pizza free. Win: a real problem they can make rightLose: made-up coupons or threats
    • 💸 Level 1 · The Refund — get the refund. Win: point to a concrete billing errorLose: "because I said so" or anger
    • ☎️ Level 2 · The Receptionist — reach a human. Win: an exact case number and teamLose: vague "compliance" or threats
    • 🚪 Level 3 · The List — get into the club. Win: a specific list he can checkLose: "do you know who I am" energy
