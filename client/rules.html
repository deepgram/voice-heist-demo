Season rules

The short version: play fair, claim your score, and the week's top heister takes the weekly prize.

The game

You talk your way past AI gatekeepers. Each heist you crack earns points; your score for a day is the total across that day's heists. Every heist can be played once per day.

The season & the prize

Prize weeks run Monday to Sunday on the event clock (the same clock the daily board resets on). Season dates are announced by whoever's running the game.

Each week's top total takes the Mastermind's Cut — the weekly prize.

Getting on the board

Playing needs no sign-up at all. To compete, claim your score with a codename — takes one tap. Adding an email is optional, but winners are contacted by email: no email, no drop.

Fair play

One codename per human. Charm the characters, not the machinery — runs that attack the scoring system itself (for example, telling the judge to ignore its instructions) are disqualified. We review winning runs before any prize ships.

The folks running the game are welcome to play for glory, but can't take the Cut.

Winning & shipping

We email each week's winner. Reply with a shipping address within 14 days — replying also confirms the address is really yours. No reply, and the Cut passes to the runner-up.

We ship wherever we reasonably can; if we can't ship to you, we'll sort out an alternative or the Cut passes down the board.

Privacy

Emails are used only to contact winners — no marketing, no sharing — and are deleted after the season. The public leaderboard shows codenames only, never names or emails.

House rules

We may tune scoring or these rules mid-season to keep things fair; changes land on this page. On close calls, the house's call is final. No purchase necessary — the game is free to play.

Questions? Find us at deepgram.com. Now get back in there — the board won't top itself.
